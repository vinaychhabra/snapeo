Frequently asked questions

Where is Snapeo Studios located?

Snapeo Studios is based in Rudrapur, Uttarakhand, India, and shoots weddings across India and abroad.

What services does Snapeo Studios offer?

Wedding photography, cinematic wedding films, pre-wedding shoots, hand-bound wedding albums and destination wedding coverage. Photography and film are handled by one small team.

How early should we book Snapeo Studios?

As soon as you have a date or a shortlist of dates. Popular dates, especially November to February, are taken first.

Does Snapeo Studios travel for weddings?

Yes. Snapeo Studios shoots across India and abroad and handles its own logistics.
