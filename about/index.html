About Snapeo Studios, wedding photographers in Rudrapur

Snapeo Studios is a wedding photography and wedding film studio based in Rudrapur, Uttarakhand. We take a limited number of weddings each season, so every couple receives our best work. Photography and film come from one calm, small team that works across Uttarakhand and travels anywhere in India and abroad.

Our approach is simple: be present, stay quiet and let the day unfold. We shoot in natural light with calm direction, and we plan each event with the couple before the wedding. Explore our portfolio and films, or check your date.
