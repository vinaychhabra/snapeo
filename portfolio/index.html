About this wedding photography portfolio

These photographs come from weddings, pre-wedding shoots and editorial portraits shot by Snapeo Studios, a wedding photography studio in Rudrapur, Uttarakhand. Use the filters above to browse by type, and tap any photo to see it larger.

  • Wedding: ceremonies and rituals from haldi and mehendi to the baraat, varmala, pheras and vidaai, shot in natural light.
  • Pre-wedding: relaxed couple sessions planned around golden hour, in the hills or at a location you choose.
  • Editorial: details, jewellery, outfits and portraits that tell the quieter parts of the story.
