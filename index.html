Why do couples choose Snapeo Studios?

We take a limited number of weddings each season so every couple gets full attention. Our style is calm direction and natural light: we guide gently, stay out of the way and let the day unfold. Because photography and film come from the same team, you meet fewer strangers and the photos and film tell one matching story.

Is a pre-wedding shoot worth it?

For most couples, yes. A pre-wedding shoot gets you comfortable in front of the camera before the big day, so wedding photos look more relaxed. It also gives you photos for save-the-date invites and a short film to play at the sangeet. Plan it 1 to 2 months before the wedding so the edited photos are ready in time for invites.

How does Snapeo Studios plan wedding coverage?

A typical North Indian wedding runs over 2 to 4 days with 4 to 6 separate events. Before the wedding we sit with the couple and build a plan for each event: who is shooting, which family members matter most and which moments cannot be missed. Smaller events like haldi and mehendi need a lighter crew, while the baraat, pheras and reception need more coverage because many things happen at once.

Light changes the plan too. Banquet halls in Rudrapur and Haldwani often have mixed indoor lighting, so the team carries its own lights for stage and night shots. For hill venues in Nainital or Mukteshwar, couple portraits are planned around golden hour, which in winter falls roughly between 4 pm and 5:30 pm.

What should you ask a wedding photographer before booking?

Ask to see two or three full weddings, not just the best single frames, so you know how the photographer handles every event and every kind of light. Check who will actually shoot your wedding and how many people come on each day. Confirm what is included: edited photos, a highlight film, a full film, an album, and how you receive them. Finally, ask how they handle travel and stay for outstation or destination weddings, so the quote has no surprises later.
